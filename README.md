| --- | --- | --- |
| **Printbot 3D**<br /><sub>in development</sub> | A 3D-printing service where customers upload STL, OBJ or STEP models, preview them in a 3D viewer in the browser, and place print orders. Includes JWT authentication, an admin dashboard, and email and WhatsApp notifications. | React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Three.js |
